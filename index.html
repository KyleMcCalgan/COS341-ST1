COS341 · Course Notes

Compiler design, organized by topic area

These standalone study notes cover the two core construction pipelines that connect a source program to a parser: lexical analysis turns characters into tokens, while parsing checks those tokens against a context-free grammar. The past-paper and homework sections apply both topic areas to complete worked solutions.
